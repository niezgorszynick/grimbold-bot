Skill Challenges, Spells on Your Friends & PDF Sheets

The DM can now run skill challenges, at the table and in Owlbear. Buff spells go on your party and change their rolls, Concentration saves are rolled for you, and fighting styles and class features add to your damage. Your sheet can also be printed on the official character sheet.

For everyone

🎯 Skill challenges

A tense chase, a river crossing, a negotiation: the party rolls skills in turn until enough successes, or too many failures.

  • Turns go in initiative order. Roll initiative on your sheet to take your place.
  • On your turn, roll one of the skills the DM allows. Each character uses a skill once per challenge. You can also 🤝 Help, which gives the next roller Advantage.
  • Rolling from your character sheet: a skill roll on your turn asks whether it's your challenge turn. Say OK and it counts.
  • You see the order, whose turn it is, and the tally, unless the DM keeps the tally hidden.

🦉 Owlbear

  • A running challenge opens in its own window. It closes when the challenge ends, and the Dice window links to it.
  • Windows you can arrange: drag a window by its title bar (an outline shows where it will land), resize it from the corner, or shrink it to its title bar with ▁. Your layout is remembered for you.
  • ⧉ Pop out the roll log from the Dice window into a window of its own.

✨ Spells on your friends

  • Casting a buff spell asks who gets it: you and anyone in a running adventure with you, up to the spell's limit (Bless: 3, one more for each higher slot).
  • The target's sheet lists it under Spells on you, and their numbers change. Bless adds +1d4 to attack rolls and saves; Shield of Faith, Haste, Warding Bond, Mage Armor and Barkskin change AC; Magic Weapon, Elemental Weapon, Enlarge, Divine Favor and Crusader's Mantle add to weapon damage.
  • Other buffs (Heroism, Fly, Stoneskin, Death Ward, Guidance, Hunter's Mark, ...) are listed with what they do.
  • When it ends: a Concentration spell ends for everyone when the caster stops concentrating. The others end with a rest (Mage Armor with a Long Rest), or with End, which the target or the caster can press.

🧠 Concentration saves

  • Taking damage while concentrating puts a save under Spellcasting: DC 10 or half the damage, one save per hit.
  • 🎲 Roll rolls your Constitution save, with War Caster or Eldritch Mind giving Advantage. If you rolled real dice, press Passed or Failed.
  • A failed save ends the spell, and any buffs it held on your friends.

⚔ Damage that adds up

  • Dueling adds its +2 to one-handed melee damage (a Rapier is 1d8+5 now, not 1d8+3).
  • Rage and Sacred Weapon have a Start button on their feature. It spends a use, and your attacks include the bonus until you press End, rest, or drop to 0 HP.
  • Sneak Attack: Finesse and ranged weapons get a second damage button, + Sneak Attack 3d6, for the turns you qualify.

📄 Your sheet as a PDF

  • 📄 Export PDF at the top of your character sheet fills in the official 2024 character sheet: abilities, saves, skills, attacks, features, spell slots, spells and equipment.
  • The fields stay editable, so you can keep it up at the table. Save your sheet first if you've just changed something.
For DMs

Running challenges

Skill challenges

  • Prepare one on the adventure table: a name, the situation, successes needed, failures allowed, a DC only you see, the skills that fit, and whether players see the tally.
  • Run it when the moment comes. The order follows initiative, and you can act for anyone and ⏭ Skip a turn.
  • Your rulings: +5 DC for a stretch of a skill, Void to take back a turn, a skill already used allowed anyway, and ✨ Spell / feature to count a clever spell or feature as a success, once per character.
  • When it's decided, end it on the table; Grimbrandt can post the ending in the adventure's thread.

At the table

  • Damage you deal to a concentrating character puts ⚠ Concentration save DC X on their party card, with a roll button for you or the player.
  • You can End a character's Rage or Sacred Weapon from the table, as with Starry Form.
