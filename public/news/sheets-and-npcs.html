Character Sheet & NPC Update

Druids can shapeshift on the sheet, your gear can carry its own names and stats, Heroic Inspiration has a home, a few gaps in older characters are filled, and Notable NPCs keep the DMs' secrets.

Character sheets

Magic items and equipment

  • Add any magic item to your own character from the Magic Items section. Items you add are marked as added by you, so the DM can see where they came from.
  • Magic Items now sit right below Equipment & Items instead of after the spell list.
  • Nicknames: rename an item on your sheet ("Ambassador" for your longsword). It keeps working as what it really is.
  • Custom weapons: a custom item can have weapon stats: Action or Bonus Action, to-hit bonus, damage dice, damage bonus and type. It rolls like any other weapon, and the editor shows the math live (for example +2 STR + 3 proficiency + 2 weapon).

Heroic Inspiration

A sunrise tile next to Proficiency Bonus shows whether you have Heroic Inspiration; tap the ⓘ for the 2024 rule. Click it to spend it. The DM awards it from the adventure table, and Humans get it back after every Long Rest.

Filling the gaps in older characters

  • A Proficiencies & Languages card lists your armor, weapons, tools and languages.
  • Characters made before lineages were tracked are asked to pick one (for example which kind of Elf), and its spells follow.
  • Circle of the Land druids choose their land in the spell section and can pick a new one after each Long Rest.
  • In the character creator you can swap your background's Origin feat for another Origin feat, if your table allows it.

  • Transform spends a Wild Shape use and gives temporary HP equal to your druid level. STR, DEX, CON, AC, speed, senses and attacks become the beast's; your HP, INT, WIS, CHA and proficiencies stay yours (saves and skills use whichever is better).
  • Every number in the form's stat block rolls, attacks included. The form ends when you revert, drop to 0 HP or take a Long Rest.
  • Circle of the Moon: CR up to your druid level ÷ 3 (CR 1 at level 3), AC at least 13 + WIS, and three times the temporary HP.

Starry Form (Circle of the Stars, level 3+)

  • Spend a Wild Shape use to take your starry form for 10 minutes: Archer (a rollable luminous arrow), Chalice (extra healing to roll when you heal with a spell slot) or Dragon (treat a d20 of 9 or lower as 10 on INT and WIS checks and Concentration saves).
  • At level 10 the dice become 2d8, the Dragon flies, and you can switch constellation each turn. At level 14 you resist bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage.
  • Cosmic Omen (level 6) is rolled after each Long Rest: Weal adds a d6, Woe subtracts one.
Notable NPCs

NPCs keep their secrets

  • Players see an NPC's name, role, portrait and the adventures they appeared in. Lore, status and stats are for DMs only.
  • NPCs can have a portrait or token, shown on the NPC tab and the adventure table.

For DMs

  • Each NPC has an owning DM and plans: 🔒 Reserved (ask me first) for NPCs with long-term plans, or Free to use (one-off), with a short note.
  • Adding another DM's reserved NPC to your adventure table shows a warning first.
  • A short personality field sits next to the lore. The adventures an NPC appeared in are tags you add from a dropdown.
  • On the adventure table you can see a druid's Wild Shape or Starry Form, revert or end it, and award Heroic Inspiration.
  • DMs can correct a character's armor, weapon, tool and language proficiencies on the Proficiencies & Languages card.
DMs: please claim your NPCs. Older NPCs have no owner or plans yet. Open each of yours, pick yourself as owning DM, set Reserved or Free to use, and add a portrait if you have one.
