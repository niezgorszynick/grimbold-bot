A Calmer Sheet

The character sheet shows less at once: explanations wait behind an ⓘ, hit points and resources have their own cards, and features fit on a few lines. After logging in you now land here, on What's new.

For everyone

ⓘ Help when you want it

  • The small print ("Tick a box to spend a use…", what a resource does, how a rule works) is hidden. Click the ⓘ next to a heading to read it; click again to hide it.
  • Your max HP breakdown is in the tooltip when you hover your hit points.

❤️ Hit Points and 🎒 Resources

  • Hit Points is its own card: your HP, the bar, exhaustion and active spell effects. Damage, healing and temporary HP sit under the bar; × Close tucks them away (your browser remembers).
  • Resources holds hit dice and rests, class feature uses and spell slots, side by side when there is room.
  • Resistances, immunities and similar defences sit on the Senses strip, which is now above Armor Class.

📜 Features at a glance

  • Class, subclass and species features are shown as small chips grouped by where they come from. Species traits with a ▾ open to show their text.

Smaller things

  • In View mode, use boxes (slots, feature uses) are quiet dots, and roll links lose their underline.
  • Adding a magic item is folded into ＋ Add a magic item until you need it.
  • Class and subclass features open on click to show their rules text, like species traits (as the text gets added).
  • On a wide screen, ▥ Slim next to Edit switches the sheet to two narrower columns; ▦ Wide brings back three.
  • Tables and bullet lists in spell, item and feature text are laid out as tables and lists.
  • DMs: the ✏️ by a player's name in the Players tab opens an Edit Player window.
  • After login you land on What's new. If you followed a link to a sheet or tab and had to log in first, you go straight there instead.
