Grimbold's Emporium · 19 November 2026

New Books on the Shelf

Options from Arcana Unleashed, Eberron: Forge of the Artificer and Ravenloft: The Horrors Within join the panel: the Artificer in full, 14 new subclasses, 9 species, dragonmarks and Dark Gifts, new backgrounds, magic items and monsters.

For everyone

🔧 The Artificer

  • Every Artificer feature and all six subclasses (Alchemist, Armorer, Artillerist, Battle Smith, Cartographer and Ravenloft's Reanimator) open on the sheet with their rules text.
  • Uses are counted on the Resources card: Tinker's Magic, Flash of Genius, Experimental Elixirs, Arcane Jolt, Eldritch Cannon, Jolt to Life and more, with their dice ready to roll.
  • Subclass spells are always prepared, the subclass tools are added, and Artificers can attune to 4 items at level 10, 5 at 14 and 6 at 18.
  • Artificers now have a spell list to choose from.

📚 14 new subclasses

Arcana UnleashedArcana Domain (Cleric), Arcane Archer (Fighter), Warrior of the Mystic Arts (Monk), Vestige Patron (Warlock), Conjurer, Enchanter, Necromancer, Transmuter (Wizard)
RavenloftCollege of Spirits (Bard), Grave Domain (Cleric), Hollow Warden (Ranger), Phantom (Rogue), Shadow Sorcery (Sorcerer), Undead Patron (Warlock)
  • Each comes with its feature text, always-prepared spells, limited uses and dice, and resistances or senses on the Senses strip.
  • A Vestige Patron chooses the Cleric domain its spells come from; a Warrior of the Mystic Arts casts Sorcerer spells with Wisdom; the Arcana Domain learns Wizard cantrips; the Arcane Archer picks Druidcraft or Prestidigitation.

🧬 New species

  • Changeling, Kalashtar, Khoravar, Shifter and Warforged from Eberron, and Dhampir, Hexblood, Lupin and Reborn from Ravenloft, with their skills, choices (a Shifter's type, a Reborn's resistance), spells and defences. Warforged get their +1 to AC.
  • Species traits on the sheet now open to the full text from the book, for every species.
  • Halflings got their missing Naturally Stealthy trait.

✨ Feats and backgrounds

  • Dragonmarks: thirteen marks, each with spells you cast once per Long Rest and its Spells of the Mark added to your class's list, plus the Greater marks, Potent Dragonmark and the Boon of Siberys. A character can bear one dragonmark.
  • Dark Gifts from Ravenloft can be taken in place of an Origin feat, with your DM's blessing.
  • From Arcana Unleashed: arcane Origin feats with a cantrip, the Adepts of each school (their spells come as your spell slots grow), familiar feats, Arcane Warrior and three new Epic Boons.
  • 31 new backgrounds, among them an heir for each dragonmarked house. The five Epic Boons missing from the Player's Handbook have their text now.

💎 Magic items

  • About 70 new items: the treasures of Arcana Unleashed (including its evolving items), the Artificer's plans from Eberron, and artifacts such as Blackrazor, the Wand of Orcus and the Demonomicon of Iggwilv.
For DMs

Monsters

  • 112 new statblocks in the Monsters tab: Ravenloft's darklords and horrors (Strahd included, with his legendary actions and regional effects), Arcana Unleashed's archmages and living spells, and Eberron's agents and dragonmarked heirs.
  • The source filter tells them apart from the Monster Manual.
