The Lineup: Who Stands Tallest

A new Lineup tab puts characters side by side on a height chart, each drawn as an outline that follows their build, species and class.

For everyone

  • 📏 Lineup tab: pick characters (your own, a running adventure's party, or everyone) and see them on a chart in centimetres and feet, shortest to tallest.
  • The outlines are drawn from your character: a staff for wizards, an axe for barbarians, a bow for rangers; horns and a tail for tieflings, pointed ears for elves, a halo for aasimar, a beard for dwarves.
  • To appear in it, fill in your character sheet's Appearance → Height (176 cm, 1.76 m or 5′9″ all work) and choose a Build (masculine, feminine or neutral) and a Hair length (bald, short, medium or long).
