Encounters, Gifts & Reservations

Give your gear to friends, put an item on hold at Grimbold's counter, join fights from your own card, and see exactly how your level was earned. DMs get a monster catalog, an encounter builder and Owlbear's initiative tracker filled in for them.

For everyone

🎁 Gifts

  • Every item and magic item on your sheet has a 🎁 button. Pick who it's for (any living character, yours too), how many, and add a note if you like.
  • Worn gear arrives put away; a magic item arrives unattuned but keeps its charges.
  • At the counter, the purchase window has "A gift for…": your character pays, theirs gets it.
  • Grimbold notes every gift in both characters' Discord threads.
The number next to an item reads have: it is how many you own, and changing it changes your sheet. To give some away, use 🎁, which asks give N of M.

🔖 Reserving at Grimbold's

Saving up for something on the shelf? Magic items, scrolls and the visiting merchant's goods can be reserved, so they survive the weekly restock.

WhatHow it works
Deposit10% of your price, paid now. It comes off the price when you buy.
Held forThe next 2 restocks, then the item leaves as usual.
OthersCan still buy it. If someone buys the last one, Grimbold refunds your deposit.
Run out or given upThe deposit stays in Grimbold's till.
LimitOne reservation per character, one reserver per item.

Your reservations are listed at the top of the shop. Grimbold announces each one in the shop channel, and the weekly restock lists what is still on hold.

Your character sheet

  • Portrait: upload one from the sheet header. It shows on the adventure table and the Players tab.
  • How was this level reached? A folded line under your name opens a table of every adventure you finished, the XP it gave, the running total and the levels it brought, plus what the next level needs.
  • Item bonuses: an item can carry its own bonus, such as +2 to History or Advantage on Religion checks, on skills, checks, saves or initiative. Mark it in use or put away.
  • Off-hand attacks: the extra attack with a Light weapon has its own row, without the ability modifier (unless Two-Weapon Fighting adds it).
  • Wood Elves have a Speed of 35 ft.
  • Delete Character: you can delete your own characters at the bottom of the sheet, by typing the name. Not while they are in a running adventure or have an open auction.

Adventures and fights

  • The Adventures tab shows who took part under each adventure, running or finished.
  • On the adventure table you can roll from your own character's card: initiative, saves and attacks.
  • When the DM runs a fight, you see everyone in turn order and how hurt the enemies look (Unhurt, Wounded, Bloodied, Down), never their numbers.
  • ⚔ Join the fight on your card rolls your initiative and puts you in the order. Leave the fight takes you out again; roll to come back.
  • Your initiative rolls also go into Owlbear's initiative tracker, on the token with your character's name (or first name).
For DMs

Running the table

Monsters

  • A Monsters tab with the Monster Manual statblocks: search, filter by type, CR and habitat, and open any statblock.
  • + New NPC from this statblock, or base an existing Notable NPC on one: empty stats are filled from it, and the NPC's card links to it.

Encounters

  • Plan encounters on the adventure table: monsters × count, Notable NPCs and party members, against the party's Low / Moderate / High XP budget (2024 rules).
  • Planned encounters are secret: only the adventure's DM and the Root DM see them. Other DMs only see that something is prepared.
  • ▶ Run puts the fight on the table: initiative order, HP buttons per creature, and the full statblock with dice. Monster rolls show in the dice bar for you only (Shift: advantage, Ctrl: disadvantage).
  • ✎ renames a creature (to match its token) or changes its AC and HP.
  • ⏸ Pause keeps a fight exactly as it is for later; End encounter closes it.
  • Party members in a fight are outlined in red on their own cards, with their initiative box.

Owlbear

  • "Grimbold's Dice" (now 1.1.1, so reload it) puts the running encounter into Owlbear's initiative tracker: connect your own key, open the ⚔ Encounter box and press Put into the initiative tracker. Creatures are matched to tokens by name.
  • Players' initiative rolls go in on their own while your copy of the extension is open.

The adventure's own secrets

  • NPC stats, HP and hidden NPCs on the table are for the adventure's DM and the Root DM, since some DMs also play. Hide an NPC until you reveal it.
  • Take over as DM when you run someone else's session; everyone sees who took over and when.
  • Speak in the Discord thread as Grimbrandt, any name, or a Notable NPC with their portrait.
  • An adventure started in the panel can be linked to an open Discord thread.

The ledgers

  • The Sales Ledger has a 🎁 Gifts log: who gave what to whom, how, and the note.
  • The shop lists all reservations; you can cancel one and refund its deposit.
