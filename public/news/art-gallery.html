Grimbold's Emporium · 23 November 2026

The Art Gallery

The art you post in Discord now hangs in the panel too: a new Art tab under Characters, with every picture filed under the characters in it and the adventure it comes from. All you need to do is say who is in it.

Posting art

✍️ Name them in the post

  • When you post a picture in the art channel, write who is in it in the same message, and the adventure if you like: Elara i Thorin — Sunken Keep.
  • First names are enough, in any form (Elarze, Thorina), unless two characters share it. Several characters in one picture are fine.
  • If you don't name the adventure, Grimbold guesses it: the latest adventure that everyone in the picture was in before it was posted.
  • Grimbold looks for new posts every 10 minutes and keeps his own copy of each picture.

🏷 Or tag it afterwards

  • Right-click the post (on a phone, press and hold it), then choose Apps → Tag art. Only you see the menu.
  • Pick the characters in it and the adventure, or press Not art for a meme or a screenshot.
  • It works on old posts too, and anyone with a panel account can use it.

A picture without any character tagged stays out of the gallery until someone tags it, so memes never end up there by accident.

In the panel

  • The Art tab shows the gallery, which you can filter by character or by adventure. Click a picture to see it full size, with a link to the Discord post.
  • Each character sheet has an 🎨 Art strip with that character's newest pictures, and each adventure table has one for its adventure. Finished adventures show how many pictures they have in the history list.
For DMs

Sorting

  • Import past messages on the Art tab reads the channel's whole history, threads included.
  • To sort lists every picture with nobody tagged. Tag the characters right there, or mark it Not art. Not art keeps everything set aside, in case one was a mistake.
  • Each picture has a Tag this image form for its characters, its adventure (a guess, none, or a specific one) and Not art.
