Your Campaign in the Calendar

Your campaign's sessions can now go into Grimbold's calendar without an adventure thread. Some of your players also play one-shots, so their sessions and yours now sit in one calendar. Grimbrandt warns about clashes, tags your players, reminds them the day before, and puts your sessions in their own calendars.

Setting it up (once)

  1. Go to the Discord channel where your campaign's news should appear and run /campaign create name: with the campaign's name. Grimbrandt will post all its session news in that channel.
  2. Add your players one by one: /campaign add campaign: (pick it from the list) player: @someone. Take someone off with /campaign remove.

Players get tagged in your announcements and see your sessions in /sessions and in their calendar links.

Booking, moving, cancelling (Discord)

/campaign set
Book a session: pick the campaign, a date and a time; hours is optional (4 by default).
/campaign move
Pick the session from the list, then the new date and time. Its length stays unless you give new hours.
/campaign cancel
Pick the session from the list.
/campaign info
The campaign's players and coming sessions, visible only to you.
Times are Polish time. Dates and times come from lists, so there's nothing to mistype. In the date box, type 22, 22.11 or sob to narrow the list. For the time, pick one or type it, for example 19:45.

What happens on each change

  • Grimbrandt posts in your campaign's channel and tags you and your players. Booked and moved sessions come with a 📅 Add to my calendar button.
  • A Discord server event is created, moved or deleted to match.
  • A day before, Grimbrandt reminds everyone in the channel.
  • Your players' calendar links update automatically, and a cancelled session is marked [Cancelled].

Clashes

If you book a time when you're already in another session, Grimbrandt warns you privately. The panel's Sessions tab shows which players are double-booked, whether the other session is a one-shot or your campaign. Warnings never block a booking: you decide.

The same from the panel

In the panel, the Sessions tab has a 📜 Campaigns card with your campaign:

  • Book a session with a date, a time and hours, and Move or Cancel any coming session.
  • Add players from a list (panel players with a Discord account) or remove one with ×.
  • Every change is announced in your Discord channel, the same as from Discord.
The card needs a panel login that's linked to your Discord account. If you don't have one, or the card doesn't appear, ask Maciej.

Your own calendar

/schedule calendar (in any channel), or the 📅 button under an announcement, gives you a private link to subscribe to in Google, Apple or Outlook. It shows your campaign and any one-shots you're in.

The other DMs and the Root DM can step in and manage your campaign if you need a hand.
