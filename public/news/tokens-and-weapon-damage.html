Grimbold's Emporium · 24–26 November 2026

Tokens, Maps and Weapon Damage

Custom weapons can deal more than one type of damage, and DMs get a token base (one place for every token, ready to copy into Owlbear by link) and a shelf for the Owlbear maps of past adventures.

For everyone: custom weapons

🔥 Damage of other types

  • A custom weapon can now deal extra damage of other types on every hit, such as 1d6 Piercing + 1d4 Fire. Open the weapon in the Inventory and press + Add damage of another type: dice and a damage type, up to three rows.
  • It works with a base weapon (a Longsword that also crackles with 1d6 Lightning) or without one.
  • The extra dice are part of every damage roll of the weapon: two-handed, Sneak Attack and the off-hand attack too. It is one roll, so on a critical hit every die is doubled, the extra ones included.
  • The roll is named for all its types, e.g. Ember Fang damage (Piercing + Fire), and the weapon's description in the Inventory shows them too.

Weapons you already have don't change until you add extra damage to them.

For DMs

🎭 The token base

A new Tokens tab under DM Tools keeps token images for the whole table. Players don't see it.

Uploading

  • Open ⬆ Upload tokens, choose the images (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB each; a transparent PNG works best) and say who they are of:
CharacterA player character, chosen from the list.
Notable NPCOne of the NPCs you can see.
MonsterA monster from the Monsters tab: one Goblin Warrior token serves every goblin warrior.
Custom nameAnyone else: a one-off villain, a mount, a summon.
  • Several images at once all go to the same character, NPC, monster or name.
  • Not sure yet? Choose I’ll choose later: the token waits at the top of the tab under To choose later, named after its file, until you open ✏️ Choose who it is of and link it.
  • Anyone can have several tokens, each with a short label: disguised, wild shape, mounted. One of them is the ★ main token: the first one, unless you tick Make it the main token on another.
  • Each token's ✏️ Edit changes its label, makes it the main one, links it to someone else, or deletes it.

Finding them

  • The tab groups tokens by kind (characters, NPCs, monsters, custom), with a search box (name or label) and a filter by kind.
  • Each kind is split into groups: humanoid races (Human, Dwarf, Elf, Goblin, Kobold…) first, then creature types (Beast, Fiend, Undead…). A monster's group comes from its statblock, an NPC's from the statblock it is built on, a character's from their species. Set any token's Group by hand in its ✏️ Edit or when uploading; it applies to all tokens of that character, NPC, monster or name.
  • They also show where you work: on the NPC's edit form, on a monster's statblock, and in a 🎭 Tokens (DMs) box on the character sheet. Each place has + Upload a token with the form already filled in.
  • A private NPC's tokens are seen only by its DM and the Root DM, like the NPC itself. If a character or NPC is deleted, its tokens stay under the name they had.

Into Owlbear

  • Every token has three buttons: ⬇ downloads the image, 🔗 copies its link, and ↗ opens it full size.
  • Press 🔗 and paste the link into Owlbear when you add the image. The link opens without logging in to the panel, so Owlbear can load it.
  • Or press ⬇ and upload the file to Owlbear like any other picture.

Anyone who has a token's link can open that picture. The links are long and random, so nobody can guess them, but share them only where you would show the token anyway.

🗺 Owlbear Maps

A new Owlbear Maps tab under DM Tools keeps the battle maps used in adventures, so a map from a past session is easy to find and use again.

  • ⬆ Upload maps: PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF images up to 50 MB each, several at once. Choose the adventure the map was used in and, if you like, the world-map marker where it happened (the list offers that adventure's markers). Give it a name and a note, or leave the name empty to use the file's.
  • Maps are grouped by adventure, with a filter by adventure and a search box. Each shows a preview, its marker, its note and size, ⬇ Download to put it back into Owlbear, and ✏️ Edit to rename it, move it to another adventure or marker, or delete it.
  • An adventure's page shows its maps in a 🗺 Owlbear maps strip, with + Upload a map ready for that adventure.
  • On the world map, a marker lists the maps used there; a map's 📍 marker opens the world map at that hex.

Every DM sees the maps; players never do. Tick 🙈 Hide on a map to keep it to the adventure's DM, the Root DM and whoever uploaded it.
