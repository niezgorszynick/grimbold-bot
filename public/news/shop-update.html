Grimbold's Shop Update

The shop got an overhaul: a counter that is always stocked, a smaller rotating magic vault, a proper scroll case, local artisans, weekly events, and auctions for selling loot to each other. Powerful items stay where they belong: in your adventures.

  • Prices follow the item's rarity (2024 rules: Common 100 gp, Uncommon 400 gp; potions and scrolls half), moved up or down a little each week. Your weekly /roll discount still applies.
  • Very Rare and Legendary items are never sold in the shop. Most Rare items aren't either. Those come from adventures.
  • Items you buy go straight onto your character sheet; magic items arrive as working magic items.

Auctions: selling your loot

  1. Put one of your character's magic items up for auction with /sell or in the Auctions tab of the panel. It leaves your sheet while the auction runs: 7 days.
  2. The opening bid is half the item's value. Each new bid must beat the last by 5% (at least 1 gp).
  3. Bidding takes the gold from your purse straight away. If someone outbids you, you get it back.
  4. Adventurers from out of town sometimes bid too, so the price can rise even without other players. Or it may not.
  5. At the end the highest bidder gets the item on their sheet and the seller gets the bid, less Grimbold's 10%. If nobody bids, Grimbold buys it for the opening bid and puts it on his shelf.
  6. You can withdraw your item as long as nobody has bid on it.

For DMs

What's yours to run

Master Catalog

  • Every item now has a rarity and an availability: Shop or Adventure only. Adventure-only items never appear in the shop.
  • Rare items start as adventure only. If a Rare item suits the shop (a Potion of Invisibility, +2 ammunition), open its edit page and switch it to Shop. Very Rare and Legendary can't be switched.
  • The Review banner lists items whose rarity, price or level gate looks off, with the fix shown as old → new. Untick anything you disagree with, then apply.

Restock Engine tab

  • Set the party level by hand, or tick Work it out from the characters (the median level of living characters).
  • Choose how many Common, Uncommon and Rare items the vault holds, how many scrolls the case gets, the price swing, and the weekly event chance (40% to start).
  • Items you put on the shelf yourself in the Shop Items tab now stay until they sell or you delete them.

Artisans

  1. Restock Engine tab → Artisans → type a name (e.g. Benet the Leatherworker) → Add artisan.
  2. Open them and add their goods: name, category, price in gp, how many they make per week, and the most that can sit on the shelf.
  3. Untick Supplying Grimbold while they're away (say, after something happened to them in an adventure). Their goods leave the shelf at the next restock.

Weekly events

Each restock, there's a chance one event fires. Events with a higher weight are picked more often. Grimbold reads the event's announcement out in the restock post.

Visiting merchantSells 1–3 of their goods for a week. Goods go one per line: Name | price in gp | category | description
ShortageNo new scrolls, or no new magic items, that week.
SaleA % off the mundane counter, staples, scroll case or artisan goods.
News onlyJust a line in the restock post.

Example merchant line: Dwarven Chain Shirt | 60 | Armor | Fine dwarven links

Please add events and artisans tied to your adventures. The list starts empty, and every event you write makes the shop a little more alive. Did your party save a caravan? Its merchant can visit. Did a smithy burn down? Run a shortage. Did the town win a festival? Hold a sale. Write the announcement as Grimbold would say it.
