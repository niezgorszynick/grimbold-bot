Sessions, Calendars & DM Tools

Session dates now live in one place. Answer the DM's poll with a button, get tagged and reminded, and see every session in your own calendar, with a warning if you're in two games at once. Campaigns outside the adventure threads share the same calendar. DMs get 5etools imports, homebrew statblocks and hidden monster rolls.

For everyone

Picking a date

When your DM proposes times, Grimbrandt posts a poll in the adventure's Discord thread with up to four times, each shown in your own clock.

  • Under each time, click ✅ can, 🤔 maybe or ❌ can't. Click again to change your answer; the newest one counts.
  • If you're already in another session at that time, Grimbrandt tells you privately. Your answer still counts, so let the DMs know if you'd rather swap.
  • The DM locks one time in, and the poll shows the chosen time and who's coming.

Once a date is set

  • Grimbrandt posts it in the thread and tags everyone in it: those who said ✅, the DM and the party.
  • A Discord server event appears too. Click Interested if you want Discord's own reminder.
  • If the date moves or the session is cancelled, Grimbrandt posts that in the thread, and the event follows.
  • A day before, Grimbrandt posts a reminder and tags everyone in the session.

📅 In your own calendar

Get your sessions in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Changes and cancellations update there automatically.

📅 Add to my calendar
The button under each session announcement in Discord.
/schedule calendar
Works in any channel.
Sessions tab
In the panel: Show my calendar links.
  • Each shows your own links, visible only to you. Pick your calendar app and subscribe once.
  • Your calendar holds the sessions you said ✅ to, the ones you DM, the ones your character is in the party for, and the sessions of any campaign you're a player in.
  • A moved session moves in your calendar; a cancelled one shows as [Cancelled].
  • Google Calendar can take a few hours to pick up a change. Grimbrandt's posts and the server event change immediately.
Keep your calendar link to yourself: anyone who has it can see your sessions.

Who plays when

/sessions
Sessions in the next two weeks, visible only to you. Add mine:True for just the ones you're in.
Sessions tab
In the panel: sessions by day for the next 3 weeks (or 2 months), who's in each, any clashes, and the polls still open.

In fights

The DM can keep monster rolls hidden. In the adventure table's roll log they show as 🔒 until the DM reveals them, sometimes after the fact.

Campaigns

Some of you also play in a campaign that runs outside the adventure threads. Its sessions now sit in the same calendar as the one-shots, so a clash between the two shows up for everyone. Its players get the same tags, reminders and calendar entries.

For the campaign's DM

  1. Go to the Discord channel where the campaign's news should appear and run /campaign create name: with the campaign's name. Grimbrandt will post all its session news there.
  2. Add players with /campaign add campaign: (pick it from the list) player: @someone. Take someone off with /campaign remove.
/campaign set
Book a session: pick the campaign, a date and a time; hours is optional (4 by default).
/campaign move
Pick the session from the list, then the new date and time. Its length stays unless you give new hours.
/campaign cancel
Pick the session from the list.
/campaign info
The campaign's players and coming sessions, visible only to you.
  • Each change is posted in the campaign's channel, tagging you and your players, with a 📅 Add to my calendar button. The Discord server event follows, and a reminder goes out a day before.
  • If you book a time when you're already in another session, Grimbrandt warns you privately. Warnings never block a booking: you decide.
  • In the panel, the Sessions tab has a 📜 Campaigns card for the same things: book, move and cancel sessions, and add or remove players. Changes there are announced in Discord too. The card needs a panel login linked to your Discord account; ask Maciej if it doesn't appear.
For DMs

Running the table

Scheduling an adventure

Run these in the adventure's Discord thread. The adventure's DM or any DM can use them. Times are Polish time.

/schedule propose
Up to 4 times, for example 22.11 20:00, 29.11 18:00 (also pt 20:00 or jutro 19:30). The suggestion list shows how Grimbrandt read them before you send. Pick the winner from DM: lock in a time….
/schedule set
Book a known date at once. The date and time are picked from lists; hours is optional (4 by default).
/schedule move
Move the next session to a new date and time.
/schedule cancel
Cancels the open poll, or else the next session.
  • In the panel, a running adventure's table has a 📅 Sessions section where you can book, move and cancel with date pickers. Changes from either side show on the other, and panel changes are announced in the thread.
  • Clashes are warned about (to the voter, to you, and on the Sessions tab) but never block.
  • Any DM can manage any campaign, from Discord or the 📜 Campaigns card.

Monsters

  • Import from 5etools (Monsters tab): choose a 5etools JSON file (a book's bestiary or a homebrew collection), give it a name and press Import. Its creatures join the catalog, encounters, NPC statblocks and dice. Imported files stay on the server; book text never goes into Git.
  • Book filter: chips above the list filter by source, several at once, with All / Clear. Your choice is remembered.
  • ⚔ Add to encounter on any statblock adds it to an open encounter in your running adventure, or to a new one.

Homebrew statblocks

Homebrew statblocks belong to the adventure. The shared catalog never changes.

  • ✎ Customize in the encounter planner: type a catalog monster (say, Black Bear), change it in the editor, and it's added to the encounter. The catalog keeps the original.
  • + Homebrew… adds one you've already made; + New homebrew creature… starts one from scratch. Its entries come with dice ready to roll.
  • 📜 This adventure's homebrew statblocks lists them with Edit, and Delete for ones no fight uses.

Monster rolls: hidden or shown

  • Monster rolls are stored with the adventure. Hidden ones show to the party as 🔒 in the roll log.
  • Reveal next to a hidden roll shows it to the party, in the roll log and in Owlbear, right away or later.
  • During a running fight, the dice bar has a toggle, 🔒 Monsters: hidden / 👁 Monsters: shown, for whether new monster rolls go out as you roll them. You can reveal a hidden roll either way.
