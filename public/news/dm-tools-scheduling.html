DM Tools: Monsters, Homebrew & Scheduling

Since the last update: import whole bestiaries from 5etools, write homebrew statblocks for your adventure, hide monster rolls and reveal them when you like, and schedule sessions with polls or straight booking, from Discord or the panel. Players get tagged, reminded and calendar-synced.

Monsters

  • Import from 5etools (Monsters tab): choose a 5etools JSON file (a book's bestiary or a homebrew collection), give it a name and press Import. Its creatures join the catalog, encounters, NPC statblocks and dice. Imported files stay on the server; book text never goes into Git.
  • Book filter: chips above the list filter by source, several at once, with All / Clear. Your choice is remembered.
  • ⚔ Add to encounter on any statblock adds it to an open encounter in your running adventure, or to a new one.

Homebrew statblocks

Homebrew statblocks belong to the adventure. The shared catalog never changes.

  • ✎ Customize in the encounter planner: type a catalog monster (say, Black Bear), change it in the editor, and it's added to the encounter. The catalog keeps the original.
  • + Homebrew… adds one you've already made; + New homebrew creature… starts one from scratch. Its entries come with dice ready to roll.
  • 📜 This adventure's homebrew statblocks lists them with Edit, and Delete for ones no fight uses.

Monster rolls: hidden or shown

  • Monster rolls are stored with the adventure. Hidden ones show to the party as 🔒 in the roll log.
  • Reveal next to a hidden roll shows it to the party, in the roll log and in Owlbear, right away or later.
  • During a running fight, the dice bar has a toggle, 🔒 Monsters: hidden / 👁 Monsters: shown, for whether new monster rolls go out as you roll them. You can reveal a hidden roll either way.

Scheduling an adventure

Run these in the adventure's Discord thread. The adventure's DM or any DM can use them. Times are Polish time.

/schedule propose
Up to 4 times, for example 22.11 20:00, 29.11 18:00 (also pt 20:00 or jutro 19:30). The suggestion list shows how Grimbrandt read them before you send. Players answer ✅ / 🤔 / ❌; you pick the winner from DM: lock in a time….
/schedule set
Book a known date at once. The date and time are picked from lists; hours is optional (4 by default).
/schedule move
Move the next session to a new date and time.
/schedule cancel
Cancels the open poll, or else the next session.
/schedule calendar
Your private calendar link (works anywhere).

In the panel, a running adventure's table has a 📅 Sessions section where you can book, move and cancel with date pickers. Changes from either side show on the other, and panel changes are announced in the thread.

  • Each booking, move and cancellation is posted in the thread, tagging the people in it (✅ voters, the DM and the party), with a 📅 Add to my calendar button.
  • A Discord server event follows every change, and a reminder goes out a day before.
  • Clashes are warned about (to the voter, to you, and on the Sessions tab) but never block.
  • /sessions and the panel's Sessions tab show every session and open poll.

Campaigns

A campaign that runs outside the adventure threads now has its own sessions in the same calendar, so clashes with one-shots show up. Its DM starts it with /campaign create in the channel it should post in, adds players with /campaign add, and books with /campaign set, move and cancel. It can also be managed from the 📜 Campaigns card on the Sessions tab. Any DM can manage any campaign.
