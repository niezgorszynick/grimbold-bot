A Sheet You Can Play From

The character sheet now opens ready for play, spreads out over wide screens, and shows your passive senses at a glance. The panel's menu is tidier too.

For everyone

👁 View and ✏️ Edit

  • The sheet opens in View: rolling, hit points, rests, spell slots, casting and items all work, but the build (name, species and class, ability scores, skill choices, appearance, notes) is shown rather than edited, so a stray click changes nothing.
  • ✏️ Edit at the top shows everything as before. Your browser remembers which you used last.

🖥 Wide screens

  • On a big screen the sheet has three columns: abilities, saves and skills on the left; combat, hit points, attacks and equipment in the middle; features, magic items and spells on the right. Smaller screens and phones keep the layout they had.

👀 Senses

  • A new Senses card under your skills shows Passive Perception, Investigation and Insight (what you notice without rolling) and senses like Darkvision.

Smaller things

  • Reset Character now opens the creator in place of the sheet; Cancel brings the sheet back.
  • The panel's tabs are grouped: Shop, Characters, Adventures, DM Tools, Records, with the group's tabs in a row underneath. The changelog is What's new, by your name.
  • Rolls History shows the latest 10 rolls; Show 10 more or Show all for the rest.
  • In the Players tab, a character's name opens their sheet, and portraits sit beside the names.
For DMs

Characters and players

  • 🛡 DM settings moved from the Players tab to the foot of each character sheet (in Edit): owner, XP and level override, alive or dead, the Discord thread, multiclass levels and the adventures log. Name, species, class and purse are edited on the sheet itself.
  • ✏️ next to a player's name in the Players tab edits their Discord name and ID, for when an ID was entered wrong. The Discord ID column is gone from the roster.
