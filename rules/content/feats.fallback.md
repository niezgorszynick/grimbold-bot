## Boon of Energy Resistance
- Category: Epic Boon Feat
- Prerequisite: Level 19+

Summary (fallback text): +1 to one ability score (max 30). You gain Resistance to two damage types of your choice from Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic, Radiant or Thunder, and can change them after a Long Rest. When you take damage of one of those types, you can use a Reaction to send some of that energy at a creature within 60 feet, which takes damage of that type unless it succeeds on a Dexterity save.

## Boon of Fortitude
- Category: Epic Boon Feat
- Prerequisite: Level 19+

Summary (fallback text): +1 to one ability score (max 30). Your Hit Point maximum increases by 40, and once per turn when you regain Hit Points you can regain extra Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier.

## Boon of Recovery
- Category: Epic Boon Feat
- Prerequisite: Level 19+

Summary (fallback text): +1 to one ability score (max 30). Once per Long Rest, when you would drop to 0 Hit Points, you drop to half your Hit Point maximum instead. You also have a pool of d10s you can spend as a Bonus Action to regain Hit Points; it refills after a Long Rest.

## Boon of Skill
- Category: Epic Boon Feat
- Prerequisite: Level 19+

Summary (fallback text): +1 to one ability score (max 30). You gain proficiency in every skill, and Expertise in one skill of your choice.

## Boon of Speed
- Category: Epic Boon Feat
- Prerequisite: Level 19+

Summary (fallback text): +1 to one ability score (max 30). Your Speed increases by 30 feet, and as a Bonus Action you can take the Disengage action and end the Grappled condition on yourself.
